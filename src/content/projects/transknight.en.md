TransKnight is a 2D platformer built in Unity. Its core hook is transformation: the hero shifts between forms, and each form comes with its own movement and abilities.

## What's under the hood

- **Multi-form abilities** — each transformation unlocks a distinct move set, turning the core loop into choosing the right form for the situation.
- **Character state machine** — drives movement, attacks, and form switching cleanly.
- **Tree-based AI pathfinding** — for enemy and platform behavior.
